      Photo Selection Guide

      ✓Do
      Step 1 of 3

      Pick your best front-facing photo

      • Choose a clear, front-facing picture of yourself, framed from the shoulders up.
      • Don't worry about the background — we'll remove it for you automatically.
      ✕Don't
      Step 2 of 3

      A few things to avoid

      • Turning away from the camera — facing forward keeps you easy to recognise.
      • Accessories that cover your face, like sunglasses — colleagues should be able to identify you.
      • Overly playful poses — relaxed is great, but let's keep it professional.
      !Make Sure
      Step 3 of 3

      Check before you upload

      • Your photo is sharp, high-resolution and not too small.
      • Colours look natural — skip heavy edits such as filters, tints or black & white.

      Photo Placement Guide

      ✓Do
      Step 1 of 2

      Find a balanced fit in the frame

      • Position yourself proportionally — head and shoulders centred, with a little breathing room around you.
      • Use the references below as a guide. You can drag the photo and use the zoom control to fine-tune it.
      ✕Don't
      Step 2 of 2

      Placements to avoid

      • Zooming in so far that your face fills the whole frame.
      • Shrinking yourself so you look too small in the frame.
      • Moving off to one side, partly out of the frame.
